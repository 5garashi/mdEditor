I recorded new Japanese and English demonstrations of mdEditor source build 1.0.10 for Windows.

The videos show real operations: open Word documents, CSV and web pages as Markdown, edit diagrams and equations, then export to Word, PDF, HTML and PNG using Save As.

English demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FxBILEFQMQk
日本語の実演: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pXQqzLLf8FM

Guide and samples: https://5garashi.github.io/mdEditor/en.html
日本語の使い方: https://5garashi.github.io/mdEditor/

These recordings use the unmodified source build, rather than the Microsoft Store binary. The current Store build number has not been verified. Word import does not preserve colors or layout; exported diagrams and equations become images in Word. mdEditor does not calculate numerical results.
