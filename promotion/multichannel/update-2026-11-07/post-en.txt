mdEditor 1.0.22 — Math from AI Chats, Diagrams and 3D in Markdown

Better display of the math that AI chats write. Math spread over several lines that starts on the $$ line, $x $ with a space before the closing $, and math written as text inside HTML now show as math. SVG drawings written in Markdown are shown too, and go into PDF and Word.

Starts by zooming and turning a 3D model, then scrolls through the math of an English paper with the TeX beside the preview, followed by a top-to-bottom tour of the key features (key-features.en.md) and the diagram samples (diagram-test.en.md).

https://5garashi.github.io/mdEditor/en.html

Windows only, free. Markdown from AI chats is reproduced as closely as possible; not every way of writing can be fixed. PDF import is best effort too: multi-column layouts and scanned PDFs do not come back. Recorded from a build of the same commit as the 1.0.22 release on Microsoft Store.

Developed and produced with AI assistance (Claude, Codex). The music was composed and generated with Codex.
