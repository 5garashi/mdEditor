Better display of the math that AI chats write. Math spread over several lines that starts on the $$ line, $x $ with a space before the closing $, and math written as text inside HTML now show as math. SVG drawings written in Markdown are shown too, and go into PDF and Word.

Open PDF and Word files as Markdown, fix them, and share them as PDF or Word. Mermaid, Graphviz, math, 3D models and maps, offline. No ads, no tracking, no account.
