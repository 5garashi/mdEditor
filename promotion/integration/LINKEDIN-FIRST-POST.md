I built mdEditor, a free Markdown editor for Windows, at 5garashi.com Design Office.

I wanted a dedicated app for opening and reading Markdown files, making small edits, and sharing the result as a PDF.

mdEditor brings editing and a rendered preview together, including diagrams, equations and tables.

You can watch a 40-second demonstration and download sample documents here:
