20 main features

  1. Open PDF and Word files as Markdown, offline
  2. Headings, lists, tables, code, and pictures come back from PDFs
  3. Save as PDF in one click, looking as the preview does
  4. Save as Word, HTML, PNG, CSV, JSON, text, or a ZIP
  5. Live preview beside your text, scrolling together
  6. Markdown from AI chats reproduced as closely as possible
  7. Preview on a second display, following the line you edit
  8. Markdown colored as you type, with a check for mistakes
  9. Bold, italic, lists, tables, and heading numbers in one key
  10. Mermaid and Graphviz diagrams and math, offline
  11. 3D models (STL) and maps (GeoJSON), offline
  12. Colored code for about 40 languages, and a table of contents
  13. GitHub-style alerts, front matter, and collapsible sections
  14. Also opens HTML, e-mail, CSV, JSON, Jupyter Notebooks, and images
  15. Drop or paste pictures straight into your document
  16. Copy anything, press a key, and it opens as a new document
  17. Safe saving and automatic crash recovery
  18. Opens Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, and UTF-8 files correctly
  19. No ads, no tracking, no account; web content loads only when you allow it
  20. Keyboard, screen reader, read-aloud, and dark mode support

Preserved content depends on the format. Word import does not preserve colors or layout; notebook export omits execution output.

Your documents stay on your PC.

Editing, diagram and equation rendering, and PDF export happen locally. mdEditor itself does not collect or transmit documents or personal information.

Internet images, video and maps load only with permission. Open URL fetches the specified https page. The server receives information such as your IP address. WebView2 updates and diagnostics follow Microsoft settings.
