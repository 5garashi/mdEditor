Recording notes

Internet loading was allowed for this recording. Word import does not preserve colors or layout. Diagrams and equations export to Word as images. No automatic numerical calculation.

Main features

  1. Live preview beside your text, scrolling together
  2. Preview on a second display, following the line you edit
  3. Also a viewer: the preview fills the window
  4. Mermaid and Graphviz diagrams and math, offline
  5. 3D models (STL) you turn with the mouse
  6. Maps (GeoJSON), with OpenStreetMap when you allow it
  7. Colored code for about 40 languages, kept in PDF and Word
  8. Table of contents from your headings, updated with one press
  9. GitHub-style alerts, front matter, and collapsible sections
  10. Videos play right in the preview
  11. One-click PDF that looks exactly like the preview
  12. Opens Word, HTML, e-mail, CSV, JSON, Jupyter Notebooks, and images
  13. Saves as HTML, PNG, Word, CSV, JSON, Jupyter Notebook, or text
  14. Drop or paste pictures straight into your document
  15. Safe saving and automatic crash recovery
  16. Opens Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, and UTF-8 files correctly
  17. Internet images, videos, and maps load only when you allow them
  18. No ads, no tracking, no account
  19. Everything from the keyboard, with custom shortcuts
  20. Screen reader support, read-aloud, and dark mode
