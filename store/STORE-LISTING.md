## English (en-US)

**Product name:** mdEditor by 5garashi.com

**Short description (one line):**
Write plain text. Get diagrams, math, and a polished PDF. Private, offline, ad-free.

**Description:**

A Markdown editor that shows the finished page beside your text as you type. Diagrams, math, and tables appear as you write them, and one click saves a PDF. Your documents stay on your PC, with no ads and no account.

Write and see
- Type on the left and the preview on the right updates at once. Both sides scroll together.
- Draw flowcharts and Gantt charts (Mermaid), Graphviz diagrams, and formulas (KaTeX) with a few lines of text.
- Turn 3D models (text STL) with the mouse, and draw maps (GeoJSON, TopoJSON) on a simple world map, all offline.
- Code is colored for about 40 languages, and Contents builds a table of contents from your headings.
- Reads Markdown the way GitHub does: alerts, front matter, math blocks, emoji short names, collapsible sections, and more. Videos in a document play too.
- Put the preview in its own window on a second display. It keeps showing the line you are editing.
- Also a viewer: expand the preview to fill the window and read without the editor.

Open and save
- Save a PDF that looks exactly like the preview, diagrams and math included, on A4 or Letter.
- Open Word, HTML, web pages, e-mail (.eml), CSV, TSV, JSON, XML, Jupyter Notebooks, and images as Markdown. The original file is never changed.
- Save As also writes HTML, PNG, Word, CSV, TSV, JSON, Jupyter Notebook, and plain text, and a ZIP for sharing that holds the document and its name_files folder.
- Drop or paste pictures into the editor; they are saved beside your document.
- New to Markdown? Open a built-in sample, or press F1 and insert examples with one click.

Your work stays safe
- Saving never damages your original file, even if something goes wrong mid-write.
- Unsaved changes are backed up automatically and come back after a crash.
- Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, and UTF-8 are detected, so older Japanese files open correctly.

Private and quiet
- The content of your documents is never sent anywhere. No tracking, no accounts, no ads.
- Internet images, videos, and detailed maps load only when you allow them.

Made for everyone
- Everything works from the keyboard, and shortcuts can be changed.
- Works with screen readers and reads your document aloud with the voices on your PC. BIZ UD typeface, adjustable text size and line spacing, contrast themes, and dark mode.

Available in Japanese and English. Made by 5garashi.com Design Office. Open About in the app for the company, support, and privacy policy links.

- 3D モデル（テキスト形式の STL）はマウスで回して確かめられ、地図（GeoJSON・TopoJSON）は簡単な世界地図の上に描きます。どちらもオフラインで表示します。
- 約 40 の言語のコードを色分けし、「目次」ボタンで見出しから目次を作れます。
- GitHub と同じ書き方に対応します（注意書き、フロントマター、数式のコードブロック、絵文字の短縮名、折りたたみなど）。文書の中の動画も再生できます。
- プレビューだけを別のウィンドウにして、2 台目のディスプレイに表示できます。編集している行を表示し続けます。
- ビューアーとしても使えます。プレビューをウィンドウいっぱいに広げれば、編集欄なしで読めます。

書いたものを守る

静かで、安心
